Privacy Policy

At The Film Estate, we honor your privacy with the same devotion and care we put into crafting your dream Bengali wedding.

← Return to Home Last Updated: August 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

The Film Estate ("we," "our," or "us") respects the privacy of our clients, couples, event guests, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, safeguard, and disclose personal information gathered when you visit our website or engage our luxury wedding planning, decor, photography, catering, and event management services in West Bengal and beyond.

By interacting with our website or booking our services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To plan and execute your event seamlessly, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Details: Full name, phone number, email address, postal address, and WhatsApp contact details when you submit an enquiry or fill out our contact forms.
  • Event Information: Expected wedding date, venue details, guest count, package preferences, dietary requirements, and specific Bengali ritual customization requests (e.g., Ashirbad, Gaye Holud, Shubho Drishti, Bou Bhat).
  • Media & Event Content: Photographs, cinematic films, drone footage, and audio recordings collected during pre-wedding sessions and event coverage.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and browsing interaction metrics captured via cookies to enhance user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Service Delivery: Planning, designing, decorating, coordinating vendors, and executing your wedding or special event.
  • Client Communication: Responding to enquiries, sending customized proposals, event itineraries, contract agreements, and invoice reminders.
  • Portfolio Showcase: Displaying select highlight photos or cinematic film teasers on our website, social media channels, or promotional materials (subject to client preferences).
  • Website Enhancement: Analyzing site traffic to refine site speed, user navigation, and online enquiry workflows.

4. Media Rights & Client Privacy Preferences

We understand that weddings are deeply personal. While cinematic wedding coverage and artistic photography form an essential part of our creative portfolio, we respect your privacy choices:

If you prefer your event footage or photos to remain private and excluded from public portfolio showcases (social media, website gallery, or marketing materials), please inform us in writing at the time of contract signing.

5. Information Sharing & Third-Party Partners

We strictly do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share information only in the following necessary circumstances:

  • Vetted Event Vendors: Sub-contractors essential to your wedding setup (such as flower decorators, caterers, lighting crews, venue staff, or specialized artists) under strict confidentiality.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by applicable laws of India or court orders to protect our legal rights or prevent fraud.

6. Data Security

We implement robust physical, digital, and procedural safeguards to protect your personal details and media files against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Access to client project files is restricted solely to authorized members of The Film Estate team.

7. Cookies Policy

Our website utilizes standard session and analytical cookies to improve website responsiveness and remember user preferences. You can configure your internet browser to disable cookies; however, some interactive website features may operate with reduced functionality.

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to update/delete your personal details from our records, please reach out to us:
