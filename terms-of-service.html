2. Scope of Services

The Film Estate specializes in luxury wedding management and cultural event orchestration, with emphasis on traditional Bengali wedding rituals (Ashirbad, Gaye Holud, Shubho Drishti, Mala Badal, Saptapadi, Bou Bhat). Our core service deliverables include:

  • Event Planning & Management: Timeline creation, vendor management, guest assistance, and full-day event coordination.
  • Luxury Décor & Floral Setup: Stage setup, mandap creation, floral installations, entry gates, and ambient lighting.
  • Photography & Cinematography: High-definition wedding photography, drone footage, highlight teaser films, and custom print albums.
  • Bengali Gourmet Catering: Authentic multi-course Bengali menus prepared and served by professional culinary teams.

3. Booking, Payment Terms & Packages

We offer tailored wedding packages (including Basic, Gold, Premium, Be Unique, and Custom Packages). Payment schedules operate under the following standard guidelines:

  • Advance Deposit: A non-refundable booking deposit (as specified in your individual contract proposal) is required to secure your event date on our calendar.
  • Payment Schedule: Remaining balances are payable in milestone installments prior to the main event date. All final payments must be cleared before the event day unless explicitly agreed in writing.
  • Taxes & Additional Costs: Any additional requests outside the agreed package scope (e.g., extra event hours, custom prop additions, or venue overtime fees) will be billed separately.

4. Cancellations & Rescheduling Policy

  • Client Cancellations: If an event is cancelled by the Client, booking deposits remain non-refundable to cover pre-event vendor commitments, venue reservations, and initial administrative preparation.
  • Rescheduling: Event date transfers are subject to venue and team availability. We will make every reasonable effort to accommodate rescheduled dates without additional penalties if notice is given at least 30 days prior.

5. Client Responsibilities & Venue Permissions

The Client is responsible for:

  • Obtaining necessary permissions, security clearances, and power supply arrangements from event venue owners.
